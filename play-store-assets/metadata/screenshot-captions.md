Target size: `1024 x 500 px`

Format: JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha.

Concept: dark futuristic BreakSignal composition with the circular timer/signal mark, subtle pause bars, calm cyan/violet glow, and a clean app-preview layout.


Keep the text minimal. Do not include Google Play badges, fake awards, ranking claims, pricing claims, medical claims, or large text blocks.

## Capture Checklist

- Use the release-candidate Android build.
- Use portrait phone screenshots.
- Keep the status bar clean.
- Keep personal notifications, unrelated apps, and personal data out of frame.
- Confirm Android browser-only UI is hidden.
- Confirm screenshots are not blurry, stretched, or clipped.
- Confirm no medical guarantee wording appears.
- Confirm no Google Play badge appears inside the images.
- Verify current Google Play screenshot dimensions and quantity requirements in Play Console before upload.
