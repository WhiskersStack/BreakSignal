# BreakSignal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

Public policy URL: https://www.break-signal.com/privacy.html

Contact: support@break-signal.com

## App Overview

BreakSignal is a local-first break reminder app for eye breaks, stretching, walking, posture checks, and focus resets while working at a computer. It runs locally while the browser tab or Android app is open.

BreakSignal does not require an account, backend service, database, analytics service, advertising SDK, or external API for its core timer behavior. BreakSignal is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## Information We Collect

BreakSignal does not collect personal information. It does not ask for a name, email address, location, contacts, photos, payment information, or account credentials.

## Local Storage

BreakSignal uses browser or Android WebView `localStorage` to save local app preferences and break activity records on the user's device. This may include:

- Reminder interval and snooze settings.
- Selected break types, preset, and custom break messages.
- Theme, compact mode, sound, volume, and alert tone preferences.
- Daily break count, focus streak, and recent break history.
- Browser notification preference, where supported.

This information stays on the user's device. BreakSignal does not send `localStorage` data to a BreakSignal server.

## Notifications

On supported web browsers, BreakSignal can request notification permission only after the user enables browser notifications. Notifications are used only for break reminders while the app is open and allowed by the browser.

The Android app currently disables the web notification setting inside the Capacitor app shell and does not currently use native push notifications.

## Sound Alerts

BreakSignal can play optional reminder sounds. Sounds are generated locally with the Web Audio API. The app does not record audio, access the microphone, or load external audio files.

## Third-Party Services

BreakSignal does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, crash reporting SDKs, authentication providers, external APIs, or social media SDKs for core timer behavior.

The hosted website may be served by an infrastructure provider that processes standard connection information, such as IP address, browser details, and request logs, under that provider's own policies.

## Data Sharing

BreakSignal does not sell, rent, share, or transfer personal information. It does not transmit local timer settings or break history to the developer or third parties.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Local settings and history remain on the user's device until removed. Users can remove local data by:

- Clearing break history inside BreakSignal.
- Clearing browser site data or Android app data.
- Uninstalling the Android app.

BreakSignal cannot recover local data after it is removed because it has no account system or server-side copy.

## Android Permissions

The Android app currently declares the Internet permission. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or file storage permissions.

## Children's Privacy

BreakSignal is a general productivity tool and is not specifically directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes To This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as BreakSignal changes. The public version is available at https://www.break-signal.com/privacy.html.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@break-signal.com.
