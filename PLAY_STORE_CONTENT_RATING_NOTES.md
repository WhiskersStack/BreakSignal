# BreakSignal Content Rating Notes

These notes are for manually completing the Google Play content rating questionnaire. Review the final questionnaire wording before submission.

## Expected Content Profile

The current BreakSignal release candidate contains:

- No violence
- No sexual content or nudity
- No profanity
- No gambling or simulated gambling
- No controlled-substance content
- No user-generated content
- No social or chat features
- No purchases or in-app purchases
- No advertising
- No location tracking
- No account system
- No medical treatment or diagnostic claims

## App Purpose

BreakSignal is a general productivity timer that reminds users to pause for eye rest, stretching, walking, posture awareness, and focus resets.

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat conditions, and does not guarantee health outcomes.

## Questionnaire Guidance

- Answer based on the exact final release candidate.
- Do not infer a rating from this document alone.
- Do not submit the questionnaire automatically.
- Re-review these notes if the app adds external links, social features, purchases, ads, health integrations, or user-generated content.
