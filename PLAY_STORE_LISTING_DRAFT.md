## App Name

BreakSignal

## Short Description

Healthy break reminders for eyes, posture, stretching, walking, and focus.

## Full Description

BreakSignal helps you remember to pause during long computer sessions.

Set a calm reminder rhythm for eye breaks, stretching, walking, posture awareness, and focus resets. When it is time to pause, BreakSignal presents a clear reminder so you can complete the break, snooze it briefly, or skip it and continue working.

Features include:

- Simple break reminder timer
- Eye, stretch, walking, posture, and focus reminders
- Quick presets and custom reminder intervals
- Optional sound alerts
- Daily break counter and recent history
- Multiple dark visual themes
- Local-first settings and history
- No account, ads, analytics, or cloud database

BreakSignal is designed as a calm, dark, minimal productivity tool. Your settings and recent break history stay on your device.

BreakSignal supports healthier work habits and posture awareness, but it is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
